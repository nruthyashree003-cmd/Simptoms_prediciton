## What's Working Well
- The two attempt-1 priorities are addressed: the mobile “Explore library” control uses `white-space: nowrap`, and the wordmark now has a subtle black left border.
- The monochrome palette, ordinary Arial type, restrained heading scale, outlined controls, and light-gray surfaces remain consistent with the brief.
- At the requested 375px mobile width, all three navigation tabs stay on one line; the urgent-symptom controls remain visible and wrap within the panel.
- At 768px the two-column checker layout remains usable, while the 1440px view is centered and does not stretch the content excessively.
- The mobile recent-check rows retain distinct dates, symptoms, and View/Edit/Delete actions, and the footer fits without horizontal scrolling.
- The urgent-symptom group is emphasized with a plain black outline and label rather than color; the app's loading state resolves into the symptom list and result placeholder.

## Issues Found
No blocking issues were identified at the requested viewport sizes. The overall design is still intentionally generic, but that is consistent with the beginner-built app direction; the new wordmark rule provides enough restrained identity for this iteration.

## Priority Fixes for Next Attempt
No priority revisions remain. Preserve the simple monochrome treatment and the mobile header sizing if making future changes.

## Should the next attempt REFINE or PIVOT?
**REFINE, only if further changes are needed.** The direction is working and the two priority fixes are effective; a pivot would move away from the brief's plain, accessible student-project aesthetic.
